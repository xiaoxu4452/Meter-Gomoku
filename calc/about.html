这是什么What this is

一个完全在浏览器里运行的 15×15 五子棋分析器。Rapfi NNUE 引擎通过 WebAssembly 在本机计算， 不需要服务器，也不会把棋局上传到任何地方。棋盘、胜率、分析路线与评估曲线都在一屏内完成。

A 15×15 Gomoku (five-in-a-row) analyser that runs entirely in your browser. The Rapfi NNUE engine is compiled to WebAssembly, so nothing is uploaded and no server is involved. Board, win rate, main line and evaluation curve all live on one screen.

引擎与规则Engine & rules

EngineRapfi NNUE · WASM
线程Threads1–8 （需隔离）(isolated)
棋盘Board15×15
哈希Hash64–1024 MB
  • 无禁手：连成五子即胜，长连也算胜。
  • 长连不赢：双方必须正好五连。
  • 连珠：黑方有三三、四四、长连禁手，白方无禁手。
  • Freestyle: five or more in a row wins.
  • No overline: exactly five wins for both colours.
  • Renju: Black is bound by double-three, double-four and overline rules; White is not.

「计算分析」与「平衡点」都是持续加深的搜索：深度、节点与用时一直累加，直到你停止或改局面。

Both Analyse and Balance keep deepening: depth, nodes and time accumulate until you stop or change the position.
